Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about ATS resumes, privacy, languages, and offline use.

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

It is a resume with a clear structure, readable text, and conventional sections that make information easier for recruiting systems and people to interpret.

Does the score guarantee approval by an ATS?

No. The ATS-Friendly Score is an offline heuristic based on good practices. Recruiting systems may use different rules, and hiring decisions always involve additional factors.

Can I create a resume without internet?

Yes. The core editor, local saving, templates, score, preview, and PDF generation are designed to work offline.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The current app works without registration or login, and resumes remain stored on your device.

Can I write my resume in any language?

Yes. The interface supports four locales, while the content you type is yours and can be written in the language you need.
